## Basic Details

### Team Name:

### Team Members

* Member 2: Ashya S- RIT Kottayam

### Project Description

BreakPoint is a browser-based brick breaker game that gives players the illusion of progress while secretly ensuring that victory remains forever out of reach. Every hit damages the bricks visually, but no brick ever truly breaks.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Gamers have been suffering from a severe shortage of unwinnable games. Modern games reward effort, skill, and persistence, creating unrealistic expectations about life.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We created BreakPoint, a game that rewards your dedication with false hope. Bricks crack, scores rise, progress bars fill up, motivational messages appear, and yet the wall survives. Forever.
